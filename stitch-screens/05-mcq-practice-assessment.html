Rigorous Mathematical Derivation IS 456:2000 — Clause 38.1 Note

According to Clause 38.1 (Note) of IS 456:2000, the limiting value of xu,max / d is calculated by equating maximum compressive strain in concrete (0.0035) and steel strain at yield (0.002 + 0.87fy / Es) with Es = 2.0 × 10⁵ N/mm²:

xu,max / d = 700 / (1100 + 0.87fy)
fy = 415 ➔ 700 / (1100 + 361.05) = 0.479 ≈ 0.48
Related Identity:
xu,max / d = 0.0035 / [0.0055 + 0.87fy / Es]
