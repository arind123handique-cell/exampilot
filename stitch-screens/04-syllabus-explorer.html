RCC — Limit State Design

Fundamental philosophy, safety factors, strain compatibility, and ultimate limit state equations for flexural members.

REVISION BLOCK 01

The Limit State Method (LSM) is a semi-probabilistic design approach that ensures acceptable probabilities that the structure will not become unfit for use. It accounts for safety at ultimate loads (collapse) and serviceability at working loads (deflection, cracking, vibration).

Concrete Characteristics γm = 1.50
fcd = 0.45 fck

Accounts for 0.67 factor for size effect in structures, then divided by partial safety factor of 1.50.

Steel Reinforcement γm = 1.15
fsd = 0.87 fy

Strict quality control in industrial manufacture allows a lower partial safety factor compared to site-cast concrete.

Core Postulate

Maximum strain in concrete at outer compression fiber is taken as 0.0035 in bending, irrespective of concrete grade.

Refer IS 456:2000 Cl. 38.1(b)
error
Common Trap In Exams

Do not confuse characteristic strength fck (5% tolerance) with design strength 0.45 fck in stress block calculations. 0.67 is structural reduction!
